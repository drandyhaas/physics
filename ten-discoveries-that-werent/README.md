# Ten Discoveries That Weren't

A nineteen-page essay on ten celebrated physics claims, from N-rays in 1903 to
LK-99 in 2023, that collapsed through experimental error — not fraud, and not
an honest fluctuation. Each case gives the theory that made the claim
plausible, the apparatus, and exactly what went wrong.

📄 **[ten_discoveries_that_werent.pdf](ten_discoveries_that_werent.pdf)**

| # | case | what it actually was |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | N-rays (Blondlot, 1903) | observer bias at the threshold of vision; Wood removed the prism unseen |
| 2 | Ether drift (Miller, 1921–33) | millikelvin thermal gradients, folded by the reduction into the second harmonic |
| 3 | ZETA's thermonuclear neutrons (1958) | beam–target neutrons from instability-driven deuterons, betrayed by their energy asymmetry |
| 4 | Weber-bar gravitational waves (1969) | post-hoc thresholds, a time-zone mismatch and a code error |
| 5 | The 17 keV neutrino (1985) | detector response tails and narrow-window fits; killed by a planted ¹⁴C kink |
| 6 | Cold fusion (1989) | calorimetry artifacts, a misidentified gamma line, and 10⁸ too few neutrons |
| 7 | The Θ⁺ pentaquark (2003) | background shapes, kinematic reflections, tuned cuts and an ignored trials factor |
| 8 | Superluminal neutrinos (OPERA, 2011) | a badly seated timing fibre, 73 ns |
| 9 | Primordial B-modes (BICEP2, 2014) | polarized Galactic dust, underestimated at a single frequency |
| 10 | LK-99 (2023) | the Cu₂S structural transition near 380 K, and magnetic impurities |

The failures sort into four families — instrument faults, unmodeled
systematics, analysis errors and observer bias — and the essay closes with
eight lessons, each now a standard countermeasure: blind the analysis, prove
sensitivity with a known signal, and treat repetition with the same systematics
as no replication at all.

## Where the numbers come from

This repository's convention is that a document's numbers come from a script
beside it. This essay's do not: it is history, and its numbers are quoted from
the papers it cites. The further-reading list says which of those citations
were checked while it was written and which were given from memory.
