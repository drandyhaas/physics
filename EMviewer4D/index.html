The sphere has radius ct about the battery, which is the only thing that changes: nothing else can respond until the news reaches it, so the field is strictly zero outside it. How this is computed

Ohm's law along the wire, with the induced field kept: the potential gradient is the battery's EMF, minus the resistive drop, minus ∂A/∂t. Integrated once around the loop those three give L İ + R I = e(t), and L is not assumed — it is the same geometric kernel the charge solve uses, weighted by t·t′ instead of 1.

The charge is linear in (e, I, İ), so three solves of one matrix give it for all time. Continuity then fixes how the current varies along the wire, and that variation is what radiates. The field comes from Jefimenko's equations at retarded time, so causality is not imposed anywhere: it falls out of the lookup.
